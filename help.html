Help Center

Find answers to common questions about browsing, accounts, watching and VIP subscriptions.

How do I find a movie?

Visit the homepage and browse the available categories. If search is available on your version of CINEVA, use the search feature to look for a movie or series by title.

Why is a movie or series not playing?

Check your internet connection and try again. If the watch link is unavailable or no longer works, the content may need to be updated. You can report the problem through our Contact Us page.

Why is the download button not working?

Download availability depends on whether a valid download link has been provided for that title or episode. A download link and a watch link may be different.

How do I become a VIP member?

Visit the VIP Room, review the available plans and follow the payment instructions displayed there. VIP access should only become active after successful payment verification.

Which payment methods are supported?

The VIP page displays the payment methods currently enabled for CINEVA. Follow the instructions shown during checkout and keep your payment reference for support.

My VIP payment was successful, but my account is not VIP.

Payment verification or account activation may still be processing. Contact support with your account email, selected plan and payment reference. Never share your mobile money PIN or password.

How do I request a new movie or series?

If content requests are enabled for your account, open the VIP Room and use the request feature. Include the correct title and any useful details. A request is not a guarantee that the content will be added.

How do I report a broken link?

Visit Contact Us and tell us the title, page and problem you encountered.

Still need help?

If you cannot find the answer here, contact the CINEVA support team.
